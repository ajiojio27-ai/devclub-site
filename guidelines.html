Community Guidelines

DevClub is for developers who build and test together. These rules keep it useful and safe.

1. Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, hate or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not with people.

2. No spam

Don't post the same thing again and again, buy followers or likes, or pester people with links.

3. Test for real

Open the app, use it and give honest feedback. No fake installs, fake check-ins or fake ratings.

4. No scams or fraud

Don't ask for money, passwords, codes or personal details, and don't trick people into installing anything.

5. Keep it clean

No sexual content, and nothing that shows or sexualises minors. Ever.

6. No violence or threats

Don't threaten people or encourage anyone to hurt themselves or others.

7. Be yourself

Don't pretend to be another person, company or DevClub staff.

8. Respect privacy and ownership

Don't share other people's private information, and only post code and content you have the right to share.

9. Protect younger members

Some members are under 18. Be extra careful with them, and never try to move a conversation somewhere private.

Breaking these rules can get content removed or an account suspended. See something wrong? Use Report on the post, comment or profile.

These are the same guidelines you see in the app under Settings → Community guidelines.
