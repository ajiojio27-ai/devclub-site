Delete your DevClub account

This page explains how to delete your DevClub account and the data that goes with it. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in anywhere else to ask.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open DevClub and go to Profile → Settings (gear) → Delete account.
  2. Read what happens, type DELETE, and sign in with Google again to confirm.
  3. Your account is deactivated at once and you are signed out.

Option 2: you can't open the app

Lost your phone, or the app won't open? Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the Gmail address of your DevClub account with the subject "Delete my DevClub account". We deactivate the account, usually within a few days, and reply to confirm. We only act on requests that come from the account's own address.

What happens next

Right away: your account is hidden (search, feeds, profiles, chat lists), you leave any queue or squad without strikes, and your phone stops getting notifications.

15 days to change your mind: sign in to DevClub and tap Restore account and everything that can be restored comes back. Or tap Continue deletion.

After 15 days, permanently deleted: your profile and private data (including your birth year), posts, comments, likes, saves, follows, connections, requests, notification settings, devices, uploaded pictures, and your sign-in.

What stays, without your name

  • Your messages in chats lose their text and photos and show as "Deleted account".
  • Feedback you gave other developers stays, shown as "Former member", because they need it.
  • Squad history other developers rely on stays (your apps appear as "Deleted app").
  • Moderation records and reports stay for safety, without profile data.

Details are in our Privacy Policy.
