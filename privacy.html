Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]. This is the policy for the DevClub app and this website.

DevClub is a social app for developers: a distraction-free feed, chat, and a way to find real testers for Google Play's closed-testing rule. This policy says what we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it. We keep it short and plain on purpose. Questions: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

1. What we collect and why

DataWhy we need it

Your profile: username, country, skills, what you use DevClub for, and your birth yearThe profile is shown to other developers (except your birth year). Your birth year is private and used only for age rules (13+ and the extra protections for under-18 members). It is never shown to anyone.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • There are no ad SDKs and no third-party trackers in the app or on this website. The website sets no cookies and loads no external fonts or scripts.
  • We do not read your other apps, your contacts, your location or your files. The app only asks for what a feature needs (photos you choose to attach, notifications you allow).

3. Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google / Firebase provides Google sign-in, push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and crash reports (Crashlytics).

They process data for us and under their own security and privacy terms. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored encrypted at rest by these providers. Direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted yet.

4. Who can see what

  • Your profile and posts are visible to other signed-in developers, unless your account is private (then only people you approve, and your connections, see your posts).
  • Your chat messages are visible to the people in that chat. If someone reports a message, our moderators can read it.
  • When you join a squad, the other members see your public profile and, to run the test, the Gmail address you consented to share for adding testers to a closed test.
  • Your birth year, e-mail address, push token and time zone are never shown to other members.

5. How long we keep data

  • Your data is kept while your account is active.
  • Photos of deleted posts, and of chat messages deleted for everyone, are removed from storage about 7 days after deletion.
  • When you delete your account, see below.
  • We keep moderation records and reports (without profile data) as long as needed for safety, to prevent abuse and to meet legal duties.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. You type DELETE and sign in with Google again to confirm.

  • Your account is deactivated immediately: it disappears from search, feeds, profiles and chat lists, you leave any queue or squad (no strikes), and your devices stop getting notifications.
  • You have 15 days to change your mind: sign in and tap Restore account.
  • After 15 days it is permanently deleted: your profile and private data (including birth year), posts, comments, likes, saves, follows, connections, requests, notification settings, devices, pictures and your sign-in are deleted.
  • What stays, without your name: your messages in other people's chats lose their text and photos and show as "Deleted account"; feedback you gave other developers stays, shown as "Former member"; squad history other developers rely on stays (your apps appear as "Deleted app"); moderation records and reports stay for safety.

If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account without the app.

7. Children and teens

DevClub is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we delete the account. Members under 18 get extra protection: an adult cannot send them a connection request unless they follow that adult first, and adults cannot start private chats with them without a connection the teen accepted. Please report anything that makes you uncomfortable.

8. Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, delete your account (above), and ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by writing to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the Google account you use. Depending on where you live you may have further rights (for example under the GDPR or similar laws), including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
